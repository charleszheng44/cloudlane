# Independent visual and accessibility review — v2

Reviewed 13 September 2026. Scope: `DESIGN.md`, `COVERAGE.md`, `preview.html`, `preview-home.png`, `preview-narrow.png`, and read-only installed Omarchy theme/shell sources. No earlier proposals, archived designs, other reviews, personal browser profile, accounts, or service requests were used.

**Verdict: revise the preview before treating it as a validated visual and accessibility reference.** The product direction and Omarchy appearance are coherent. Six concrete prototype defects below contradict the intended compact, complete, keyboard-accessible behavior. These are HTML observations, not evidence that a future Qt implementation has the same defects. Several correct production requirements already appear in the design; they still need a working representative surface and native acceptance checks.

## Prioritized findings

1. **P1 — Compact mode removes content and an essential playback control.** At 640×480, Library → Playlists contains four fixture playlists but `喜欢的现场` is `display:none`. The global `.card:nth-child(4)` rule below 840 px applies to actual Library and Discover collections, with no Library “show all” replacement. Below 1128 px the volume slider also disappears; the playback overflow contains quality/output but no volume or mute. This changes available functions with window width. **Fix:** let complete collections wrap or scroll; limit recommendations through an explicit collection-specific “view all” model. Give hidden volume/mute and current-track actions an accessible popover or overflow equivalent. Check all resources and player actions remain reachable at 640, 960, and 1280 px. Evidence: `preview.html` responsive rules on lines 10–12, Library rendering, and `#player-more` handler.

2. **P1 — Text scaling breaks the persistent player.** With `--base:24px`, the fixed 76 px player retains its original grid row while transport content grows to 76.64 px before player padding. At 640×480, the seek input extends to y=483.72 and the document grows to 490 px; the same defect occurs at 960×720 and 1280×800. Thus the required playback/seek surface is partly below the window even at a large viewport. The installed shell explicitly scales spacing with font size; the design also promises larger player minima and earlier compaction. **Fix:** derive player/header/control minima and layout thresholds from text metrics and spacing tokens, allowing an additional player row where necessary. Measure footer containment, seek usability, and visible focus at 14/18/24 px independently of display scale. Evidence: fixed grid tracks in `preview.html:8–9`; installed `shell.toml` `[spacing] scale-with-font = true` and `Style.qml` `effectiveSpacingScale`.

3. **P1 — The narrow context panel leaves fully obscured content keyboard-active.** At 640×480, open Queue and press Shift+Tab from its focused Close button: focus moves to `城市边缘的更多操作` in the underlying main content while the full-width queue visibly covers that button. Hit testing at the focused button's center returns a queue item, confirming the focus target is hidden. The design says the panel replaces content below 760 px, but the preview only paints over it. **Fix:** make the replaced content inert/hidden to focus and accessibility, give the replacement a labeled Back action, and restore the trigger on exit. Define the intermediate overlay policy as well: focus must remain visibly reachable, whether the panel is modal or nonmodal. Evidence: `#panel` rules and `openPanel`/`closePanel` in `preview.html:9–12,60–63`.

4. **P2 — Dynamic navigation and queue operations discard keyboard position.** Focus a Home card and press Enter: the detail page appears but `document.activeElement` becomes `BODY`. Focus the second queue item's Move up button and press Enter: rebuilding the panel again leaves focus on `BODY`, preventing repeated keyboard reordering. This is different from the working Escape-to-trigger path. Also, the native HTML dialog has an empty accessible name because its visible title is not associated with the dialog. **Fix:** retain focus by resource/queue-entry key across list changes, move focus intentionally to the new detail heading/main region, and restore the originating item on Back. Associate the dialog with `#modal-title`. Verify keyboard-only detail/back, category changes, repeated reorder/removal, and nested sheets; native Qt needs equivalent focus and accessible-name behavior. Evidence: `detail`, `renderPanel`, delegated move/remove handlers, and `<dialog id="modal">` in `preview.html`.

5. **P2 — Track rows are approximately 80 px, undermining the compact list layout.** At all three tested window sizes with 14 px text, the first row measures 79.66 px rather than the specified 44–48 px. The 72 px action column has 12 px horizontal cell padding, leaving 60 px for two 32 px controls; Add and More wrap onto separate lines. The Home screenshot visibly catches the first Add button above its overflow button, and the Recent section barely enters the initial 1280×800 view. **Fix:** keep actions on one line, preferably one persistent overflow with Add to queue inside it, or reserve enough width for both controls. Use a row layout whose minimum follows the two-line title/artist text and font scale. Confirm normal rows are 44–48 px at base 14 and content does not clip at larger text sizes. Evidence: `.table .action`, `.table td`, `.icon`, and `rows()` in `preview.html:7–8,34`.

6. **P2 — Meaningful dark control boundaries miss the design's contrast target.** `--line:#414868` is only **1.91:1** against `--bg:#1a1b26` and **2.05:1** against the panel. Inputs, textareas, and selects use this boundary while sharing the surrounding background, so their input area is difficult to distinguish. Decorative dividers do not all need strengthening. The source token is the palette's `muted`; installed Omarchy provides separate `[controls]` normal/focus/hover border roles, so copying the palette alone does not implement the promised control-state mapping. **Fix:** separate decorative divider and interactive-boundary tokens, map shell control roles deliberately, and apply the design's stated 3:1 readability adjustment to meaningful boundaries where needed. Keep the clear accent focus ring. Primary and secondary text already perform well at approximately 8.10:1 and 9.32:1. Evidence: `preview.html:6–7`; active `colors.toml` `muted`; active `shell.toml` `[controls]`.

## Strengths and verification limits

- Four primary destinations, a persistent player, and one context panel provide an understandable hierarchy without a crowded top-level navigation. The compact icon rail and category selectors are appropriate patterns once their content and focus behavior are corrected.
- The current palette is accurate: `#1a1b26` background, `#13141c` panel, `#a9b1d6` foreground, and `#7aa2f7` accent match `~/.local/state/omarchy/current/theme/colors.toml`. `fc-match monospace` resolves to UbuntuMono Nerd Font; the user override sets 14 px; `hyprctl getoption decoration:rounding` returns 0. Opaque content is sensible even though the user bar override is 0.5 alpha. The brighter secondary text avoids the unreadable muted-text problem.
- Ordinary NetEase phone QR authorization has a clear place in the account UI, with no developer registration, app credentials, or partnership step. Fixtures and simulated operations are visibly labeled. The coverage ledger correctly separates designed workflows, inspected source, and unverified implementation.
- Isolated headless Chromium checks used the existing Playwright installation and `file://` preview, with 640×480, 960×720, and 1280×800 at base 14 and 24 px. Both supplied screenshots were inspected. No preview, design, screenshot, or system file was changed. Native Qt accessibility, CJK IME, mixed DPI, live theme replacement, real media, and service behavior remain production checks; this review does not claim they passed.
